About Socket Head Cap Screws

Socket head cap screws are fasteners with a cylindrical head that is typically installed with a hex key. They are the perfect choice for tight spots without enough space to use a wrench or socket, making them incredibly versatile and useful in many applications.

Types of Socket Head Cap Screws

Socket head cap screws come in different types, such as flat socket head cap screw, button head socket cap screw, cap head socket screw, and hex socket head cap screw. They are made from various materials, including steel alloys, stainless steel, and brass.

Applications of Thread Cutting Screws

Socket Head Cap Screws can be used for projects ranging from home improvement to industrial manufacturing processes. Industries like automotive repair shops, construction companies, and machine shops frequently use these fasteners.
