    Drawing & Painting Art Sets

    If you’re in need of art supplies but you’re unsure where to start, then art sets are a great option for expressing your creativity. These all-in-one sets provide quality mediums and materials, all in a helpfully curated package. Whether you prefer to paint, draw, or you enjoy a little of both, we carry drawing and painting sets that will work for all.

    An art set makes a great gift for both adults and kids. They make choosing the right tools easy with everything collected in one place. Our sets not only come with high-quality paints, many include useful art tools like a palette, palette knife, and paint brushes. Some of our large art sets even come with a table easel, allowing you to work on your art piece comfortably.

    Find the perfect medium to fit your artistic vision. A watercolor paint set is great for those who appreciate delicate colors and soft brush strokes. This enduring art medium is great for beginners but also offers an interesting style for experienced artists. If you prefer acrylics, an acrylic paint set will get you started just as easily. These sets offer an array of vibrant colors for creating rich landscapes and demure portraits. For a classic look, an oil paint set is another great choice. Oil paints are a timeless medium that brings texture and character to a variety of art projects. Many of these sets come in a wood carrying case, providing a tasteful container and a way to store your set easily when it’s not in use.

    Not just for painting, Hobby Lobby also offers sketching and drawing sets. Choose from our several options that come complete with sketching pencils, pencil sharpeners, erasers, and more. Enjoy the many art sets we have waiting for you!
