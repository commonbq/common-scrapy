Hotels in Miami, FL

Miami, the vibrant coastal metropolis, is a fusion of cultures, cuisines, and lifestyles that make it a uniquely perfect destination for a trip. Known for its stunning beaches, such as South Beach, and the iconic Miami Beach Architectural District, the city offers a blend of sun-soaked relaxation and energetic nightlife. Marriott Bonvoy’s Miami hotels provide the ideal base for your adventures, with easy access to top attractions like the Wynwood Walls, the Design District, and the Biscayne NP. Whether you're here for a beach vacation, a cultural exploration, or a business meeting, the city’s diverse neighborhoods and world-class amenities ensure a seamless and enjoyable stay.


Many of our hotels offer complimentary Wi-Fi, fitness centers, and sparkling pools, providing the perfect setting to relax after a day of exploration. With properties in the heart of the city and in more peaceful areas, Marriott Bonvoy enables you to fully experience the allure of Miami, from the colorful street art to the serene coastal promenades. Near many of our hotels, you can savor the flavors of Miami at its celebrated eateries, where Latin and Caribbean cuisine meets American classics. Stroll through the verdant landscapes of Fairchild Tropical Botanic Garden or dive into the vibrant marine world at the Phillip and Patricia Frost Museum of Science.

Explore Miami

Check out our travel tips before booking your hotel.

Miami is a haven for those who appreciate the great outdoors, with its verdant tropical settings and lively ecosystems. Biscayne Bay, an extensive aquatic wonderland, invites you to enjoy kayaking, paddleboarding, and snorkeling amidst mangroves and coral reefs. A short drive away, the Everglades Safari Park beckon with their airboat tours and bird-watching excursions, offering a glimpse of the city's untamed beauty. If you prefer to stay on land, the Miami Beach Boardwalk is a delightful choice, meandering through the city and granting stunning views of the ocean and the skyline.

Miami’s commitment to preserving natural beauty is evident in its parks. Fairchild Tropical Botanic Garden is a serene 83-acre paradise of rare tropical plants, palms, and flowering trees. Matheson Hammock Park is a picturesque spot to sunbathe, picnic, or swim in the man-made atoll pool. Whether you’re seeking outdoor adventure or a peaceful retreat, Miami’s natural attractions are sure to inspire and rejuvenate you.

Miami's cultural tapestry, woven with Latin, Caribbean, and American threads, is a vibrant and unique experience. Staying at one of our hotels in Miami places you at the heart of this dynamic mix, where the streets resonate with the beats of salsa and reggaeton, and the aromas of tropical cuisine waft through the air. Discover the city's artistic soul at the Wynwood Walls, a neighborhood that's a canvas for colorful street art and home to cutting-edge galleries. Miami's rich cultural tapestry is on full display in its many neighborhoods, each with its own unique character and traditions.

A visit to Little Havana offers a taste of Cuban culture, where you can savor a traditional cup of café con leche and watch locals play dominoes in the park. In contrast, the Art Deco Historic District in South Beach provides a glimpse into the city’s glamorous past, with its pastel-hued buildings and vintage allure. Whether you're exploring museums, taking in live performances, or simply strolling through the streets, Miami's culture is certain to make a lasting impression. After a day of cultural exploration, retreat to the comfort of your Marriott Bonvoy hotel, where ocean views, rooftop bars, and spa services await to pamper the discerning traveler.

Miami's tropical climate promises sun-drenched days, creating an idyllic backdrop for beachside relaxation or outdoor adventures. The summer months, spanning from June to August, deliver balmy temperatures, often peaking in the mid-80s to low 90s Fahrenheit, with the occasional afternoon thunderstorm to cool things down. Opting for a stay at a Marriott Bonvoy property with a pool or beach access is the perfect way to savor these sultry days.

Miami's winter is a delightful escape, with temperatures hovering around the 70s. Fall and spring, from September to November and March to May, offer a comfortable range from the 70s to the mid-80s, perfect for exploring the city's vibrant neighborhoods, savoring outdoor dining, and taking leisurely strolls by the beach. Miami's climate ensures that every season is a great time to visit, and Marriott Bonvoy's Miami hotels are ready to cater to your needs, whether you're seeking relaxation or adventure.

Miami International Airport, one of the world's busiest airports, is your gateway to the city, with a wide range of domestic and international flights. Once you land, you'll find plenty of transportation options, from taxis to ride-sharing services like Uber and Lyft, to the plethora of Miami Airport shuttles, which connects to key areas across the city. Many Marriott Bonvoy hotels in Miami also offer airport shuttles, making your arrival and departure as seamless as possible. If you're driving to Miami, you'll find that the city is easily accessible by major highways and interstates. And if you're traveling by train or bus, Amtrak and Greyhound both offer regular services to Miami.

Once you're in Miami, you'll find it's easy to get around. The Miami-Dade Transit system operates buses and the Metrorail, which can take you to most major neighborhoods and attractions quickly and affordably. Taxis and ride-sharing services are also widely available, and many travelers choose to rent a car. Car rental companies are located at Miami International Airport and throughout the city, and parking is available at most hotels. Whether you prefer to take public transportation, drive or walk, Miami makes it easy to explore the city on your own terms.

Miami is a vibrant playground for families, offering an array of kid-friendly activities that will keep children entertained and engaged. Start your adventure at the Miami Children’s Museum, where interactive exhibits like the KidSmart and the Music and Movement Center encourage learning through play. For a closer look at marine life, head to the Miami Seaquarium, where kids can watch dolphins, sea lions, and killer whales perform in thrilling shows, or even participate in a behind-the-scenes tour to meet some of the park’s residents. With our conveniently located hotels, you’ll always be a short distance from these exciting attractions.

For a more natural and outdoor experience, the Vizcaya Museum and Gardens is a must-see. The sprawling gardens and hidden play areas are perfect for exploring and using your imagination. If you’re looking for a more active adventure, the Miami Beach Boardwalk is a great place to start. The scenic path is perfect for biking, rollerblading, or a leisurely walk, with plenty of places to stop and play along the way. After a day of fun, relax and enjoy some family-friendly dining at one of the many kid-friendly restaurants in the area.
