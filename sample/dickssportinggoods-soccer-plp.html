Find the Right Soccer Equipment for Practice & Play

From soccer training equipment to soccer cleats, kick this season into gear with soccer equipment from DICK'S Sporting Goods.

Shop quality soccer gear from brands you trust, like adidas®, Nike®, PUMA® and more.

Bring out your best on the field with the proper soccer gear for your game.

Work on your footwork with soccer balls in a variety of sizes and colors. Choose from thermal-bonded, hand or machine-stitched panels, and balls designed for indoor, hard floor, turf and grass.

Play hard and stay protected on the field with shin guards from G-Form, Nike® and DSG®. Shop for soccer socks in a variety of colors that will match your uniform and keep your shin guards in place.

Want to represent your favorite soccer players and teams? Find fan gear and jerseys in our soccer fan shop, plus check out the newest release of MLS kits in our 2025-26 MLS Jerseys Guide.
