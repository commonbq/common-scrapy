    Painting Supplies

    Whether you’re a beginner or an expert, Hobby Lobby is the place to stock up on painting supplies. We carry paint for use in multiple mediums, including acrylic, watercolor, and oil paints. You’ll also find many useful tools to go with those paints, allowing you to achieve your artistic vision. Satisfy your love of creating painted art with all we have to offer.

    If you prefer paints that are versatile and bring rich color to each new creation, go with acrylics. We feature tube and bottle paints that work on many surfaces, like wood, canvas, and fabric. They can be applied with paint brushes or palette knives to create unique textures. Once finished, apply a coat of gloss varnish. This protects your canvas or craft from natural wear while also giving the piece a shiny finish.

    There are also watercolor and oil paints for those who enjoy classic painting mediums. Pick up paint sets that contain everything you’ll need to get started. Our Master’s Touch fine art brand is one to look for when you need quality and consistency. A set of Master’s Touch paints is perfect for when you need a full range of color. They’re great for painting everything from luxurious landscapes to elegant portraits and vivid flowers.

    Besides paint, there are many other supplies that are handy to have around. A bottle of epoxy resin is essential for resin crafts. Items like stain remover and paint thinner are helpful when upcycling old pieces and creating new ones. Once the brushing is finished, cleaning your paint brushes is essential to preserving their lifespan. Brush cleaner helps remove extra paint from the brush head while protecting its fibers and bristles. Pick up palettes, palette knives, paint trays, and more, then get to painting today!
